How Fieldwork works

Projects
Admins and project managers create projects and add team members from the project page.
Kanban board
Open a project's tasks and switch to Board. Drag a card to another column, or use its Move menu. Moves that break the workflow are rejected.
Review before Done
Every task must pass through Review (or QA) before it can be marked Done.
Sprints & estimates
Add estimates to tasks, plan them into sprints, and close sprints to carry unfinished work over.
Reports
Project managers can view charts and download progress, velocity and workload reports as CSV.
Activity log
Every create, update and delete on a project or task is recorded with who did it and when.
